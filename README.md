<h2 align="center">📜 The Manifesto: Modern AI & Information Conservation</h2>

<div align="center">
<blockquote>
<i>"Why gamble with stochastic token prediction when mathematical constraints can collapse your search space to $|\Omega| = 1$?"</i>
</blockquote>
</div>

<details open>
<summary><b>📖 Read: The Information-Theoretic Case for Reviving GOFAI</b> <i>(Click to collapse/expand)</i></summary>
<br>

Suppose you want to construct a frontend application without manually writing raw code:

1. **Approach A (The Rule-Based / WYSIWYG Way):**  
   You supply layout, state transitions, event handlers, and interaction invariants.  
   $$\text{Information In} (X) \xrightarrow{\text{deterministic compiler}} \text{Information Out} (X) \quad [\Delta I = 0]$$  
   Information is **strictly conserved**. Every edge case is explicitly pinned. The machine does not hallucinate because it doesn't need to guess.

2. **Approach B (The Statistical LLM Way):**  
   You prompt: *"Build me a dashboard for X with a nice color scheme."*  
   You never specified what happens when the WebGL context expires, what happens on simultaneous button debounces, or how memory handles backpressure.  
   $$\text{Information In} (X_{\text{sparse}}) \xrightarrow{\text{prior probability distribution}} \text{Code Output} (X + \text{gaps filled by probability})$$  
   Here, **information is NOT conserved**. "AI" is simply a mechanism for statistically guessing details the human specification failed to provide.

### 📐 The Mathematical Axiom:
$$\lim_{\text{constraints} \to \text{complete}} |\Omega_{\text{solution}}| = 1 \implies \text{Utility}(\text{Stochastic Guessing}) = 0$$

> **The Conclusion:**  
> When you keep adding explicit constraints until the solution space collapses to one valid implementation, the need for statistical guessing vanishes.  
> 
> In the 20th century, brilliant minds pioneered **Good Old-Fashioned AI (GOFAI)** — symbolic logic, knowledge bases, and deterministic rule engines. When statistical gradient descent hit the economic spotlight, symbolic AI was largely abandoned like the mechanical loom.  
> 
> **I'm on a mission to bring it back.** Monolithic, expressive, 100% precise symbolic engines backed by deterministic knowledge graphs.

</details>
